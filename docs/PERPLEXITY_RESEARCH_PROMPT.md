# Perplexity Research Prompt: BADGRTech BAA, SOW, Analytics, And Onboarding Readiness

Use this prompt in Perplexity or another research tool. The output is for planning and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before use.

```text
Act as a senior healthcare technology operations researcher, HIPAA-aware web vendor advisor, privacy operations analyst, and small-business SaaS launch consultant.

Company context:
BADGRTechnologies LLC is an Atlanta-based company providing HIPAA-aware website operations for small cash-pay and hybrid medical practices, including med spas, dental practices, primary care, mental health, chiropractic, and PT clinics. Services include medical website optimization, Core Web Vitals, no-PHI public-form checks, NPP/privacy visibility, accessibility basics, local visibility, Risk & Trust reports, 14-day Leak & Trust Fix Sprints, Practice Care, and BAA-ready support when applicable.

Important boundaries:
- Do not provide legal advice.
- Do not draft final legal contracts as if attorney-approved.
- Do not encourage collecting PHI through public marketing forms.
- Focus on operational best practices, checklist structure, common clauses to discuss with counsel, and implementation readiness.

Research objectives:
1. Identify what a small HIPAA-aware website operations vendor should prepare before signing clients in healthcare-adjacent work.
2. Identify practical BAA workflow steps: when a BAA may be needed, what to confirm before access, what client/vendor responsibilities should be documented, and what to escalate to counsel.
3. Identify common SOW sections for a medical website Risk & Trust Scan, 14-day website fix sprint, and ongoing Practice Care plan.
4. Identify privacy-conscious analytics options and event tracking patterns suitable for a medical practice marketing site that should not collect PHI.
5. Identify onboarding and offboarding checklists for website access, DNS, hosting, forms, analytics, email delivery, Stripe/payment links, CMS/admins, and third-party tools.
6. Identify recommended disclaimers and client-facing language patterns for “HIPAA-aware,” “BAA-ready,” “no PHI through public forms,” “not legal advice,” “not a compliance certification,” and “confirm final obligations with counsel.”
7. Identify risks BADGRTechnologies should avoid in marketing claims, client intake forms, tracking scripts, AI tools, lead enrichment, and email outreach.

Output format:
- Executive summary
- BAA readiness checklist
- SOW template outline for each service tier
- Client onboarding checklist
- Client offboarding checklist
- Privacy-conscious analytics plan
- Public-form data minimization checklist
- Marketing disclaimer language examples
- Risk register: what to avoid
- Questions to ask counsel
- Sources with links and dates

Prioritize official and authoritative sources where possible:
- HHS HIPAA guidance
- OCR guidance
- FTC health privacy guidance where relevant
- ADA/WCAG official guidance
- Reputable healthcare compliance firms
- Vendor documentation for analytics/privacy tools

Make the result practical enough that a non-lawyer founder can convert it into internal SOPs and counsel-review drafts.
```
